Why MammaWorks

Go back to work without the stress of separation

Daycare doesn't have to be your only option. MammaWorks gives you a workspace and a safe care zone, side by side.

01

Work without separation

Your child can see you're close, so they settle in calmly and at their own pace.

02

Focused time back

Uninterrupted hours in a workspace, a few days a week, for work, study or your own business.

03

Small groups, familiar faces

A small group with 4 children per carer and a regular team, so your child gets to know the people who care for them.

04

A schedule that follows your child

Plan around naps, meals and moods. Need to step out to your child? They are just a few steps away.

What it is

Calm coworking with a safe care zone next door

It is a place to belong. Around you are other parents who are balancing work and little ones, so there is always someone to share a tip, a coffee or a laugh with. A community of parents, built around one simple idea: you shouldn't have to choose between your work and being close to your child.

How it works

Simple from the first day

  1. 1

    Settle in

    Bring your child to the care zone and meet the carers. Stay as long as you both need.

  2. 2

    Start working

    Move to the workspace next door and focus on what you came to do.

  3. 3

    Stay close

    Your child is a few steps away. Step out any time, and adjust your schedule when plans change.
